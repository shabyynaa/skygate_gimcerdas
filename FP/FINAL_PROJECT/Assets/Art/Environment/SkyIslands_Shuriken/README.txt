Meadow Sky Islands
A free 3D model kit from Shuriken Studio
https://shuriken.studio/kits/meadow-sky-islands

A free low-poly meadow sky islands kit for Roblox Studio: a matching set of classic grassy floating islands in several sizes for a sky world, a cosy lobby or a sky obby. The hero island, about 100 studs across, has rolling green hills, a cottage with a red roof, round leafy trees and a small lake that spills off the edge in a waterfall. Two medium islands carry a windmill farm with turning sails, crop fields and hay bales, and an orchard of apple trees with a cart and a beehive, a smaller island holds a wooden lookout tower with a telescope, and three small grassy islets are sized for jumping between. Every island has a walkable top at player scale and layers of soil and rock underneath with dangling roots. Built with extra detail as showpieces, they make a ready start for any sky map.

Using them
  Roblox Studio: Import 3D, choose a GLB, insert.
  Blender: File > Import > glTF 2.0.
  Unity, Godot, Unreal, three.js: import directly or through glTF.
